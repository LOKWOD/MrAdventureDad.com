# Search Console editorial decisions

## 2026-09-16 — MrAdventureDad.com

- **Report generated:** 2026-09-16
- **Reporting window:** 2026-08-17 through 2026-09-13
- **Property totals:** 2 clicks, 208 impressions, 0.96% CTR, average position 13.56

### Credible signal used

The Taughannock Falls family guide earned 21 impressions, 1 click, a 4.76% CTR and an average position of 7.95. The Destinations hub earned 11 impressions at an average position of 10. These are modest but coherent page-level signals that Google is testing the Finger Lakes destination cluster.

### Editorial decision

- Keep the Taughannock URL and title stable; the current page is already earning a click and does not need a cosmetic rewrite.
- Add a contextual reciprocal path between Taughannock Falls and the new Women’s Rights National Historical Park guide so a family can plan two distinct Finger Lakes days without stacking them.
- Upgrade the Women’s Rights guide with a four-site, primary-source-backed decision matrix covering the Visitor Center, Wesleyan Chapel, Stanton House and M’Clintock House.
- Link the Seneca Falls guide from the established Harriet Tubman and Fort Stanwix guides to create a clearer free National Park Service history cluster.

Before this update, the Seneca Falls guide had one contextual link from an established history guide. After it, three established guides point to the page, and the page provides a relevant onward path to the tested Taughannock guide.

### Signals deliberately not chased

Individual query variants had only one to four impressions, too little evidence for new exact-match pages or title rewrites. The sitemap report showed zero indexed URLs despite actual page impressions and clicks; that contradiction was treated as reporting lag or an API inconsistency, not proof that the site had no indexed pages. Crawl paths were strengthened without inventing an indexing emergency.

### Batch diversity

- **Data-led:** Women’s Rights National Historical Park — one focused reinforcement of the emerging Finger Lakes destination signal.
- **Authority-expansion:** Family car jump starters — a distinct commercial road-safety decision guide.
- **Exploration:** Family hotel fire-escape plan — an underserved evergreen family-travel safety problem.

No outreach was sent. No outreach opportunity was logged because the on-site decision matrix and internal topic cluster were the higher-confidence authority actions for this run.
