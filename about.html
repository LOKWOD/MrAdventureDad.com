ABOUT

Because childhood is short, weekends disappear fast, and the best family memories usually start with somebody saying, “let’s go.”

MR ADVENTURE DAD

Not a travel influencer. A dad who likes doing stuff.

Mr Adventure Dad is about making family adventure feel doable. Not perfect. Not endlessly curated. Just better weekends, smarter road trips, more time outside and fewer Saturdays lost to deciding what to do.

The goal is simple: give parents enough useful information to get moving, while leaving enough room for the day to become its own story.

THE CODE

Take the scenic road. Bring more snacks than you think you need. Stop for the weird roadside thing. Stay for sunset. Clean the car tomorrow.

EDITORIAL METHOD · REVIEWED OCTOBER 18, 2026

Sources, judgment and commercial links stay separate.

Destination logistics are checked against the named operator or public agency, with the check date on the guide. Hours, prices, closures and rules can change. Our itineraries and decision cards are editorial planning frameworks, not claimed first-person visits. We do not claim product testing, ratings, certifications or educational outcomes we have not established.

Paid links carry a visible disclosure. Manufacturer and regulatory references are available independently of affiliate searches. Original AI-assisted conceptual imagery is labeled and credited; it is not documentary proof of a place, person, product or event. Substantive corrections should identify the exact page and source through the public issue tracker above.
