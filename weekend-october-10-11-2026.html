Central New York Weekend Guide

What to Do With the Kids: October 10–11

The apple festival worth planning around, a sky ride over peak-color hills, a historic village gone gently spooky, and three smart alternatives.

This is an unusually good outdoor weekend

As of Wednesday morning, Syracuse is forecast sunny and near 73°F Saturday, then partly sunny near 68°F Sunday. Utica has a similarly dry, comfortable outlook. Canandaigua is trending sunny near 72°F Saturday and partly sunny near 66°F Sunday. Use Saturday for the longest outdoor plan or the mountain sky ride; Sunday is still strong for LaFayette, the foliage train or the farm. Watkins Glen currently carries afternoon-rain risk Sunday, so make that a Saturday-only choice and recheck every forecast Friday night.

1
Best overall · LaFayette

LaFayette Apple Festival

This is the clear local headliner: a 53-year-old festival with close to 500 juried craftspeople, a midway, pony rides, a petting zoo, horse-drawn wagon rides, agriculture displays, continuous live music and an unreasonable amount of apple food. It works because children get active anchors while adults have a genuinely deep maker market—not because everyone is expected to admire vendor tents for six hours.

DriveAbout 20–30 minutes
HoursSat. 9–6; Sun. 9–5
Early optionPancake breakfast from 7

Best version: Arrive near opening Saturday, do the animals and wagon ride before the crowd thickens, then choose rides deliberately instead of drifting into the midway. Families that prefer a shorter day can make the pancake breakfast the event and leave before lunch.

Book/check first: Review admission and parking through the official festival site. Tickets can be bought at the gate with cash or card, but some vendors are cash-only. Park only in designated lots, wear shoes for uneven festival ground and leave pets home—only service animals are admitted. Our Syracuse family day-trip guide can help with a second short stop.

2
Best full-day road trip · Mumford

Spooky Saturday at Genesee Country Village

Spooky Saturday puts seasonal programming inside the entire 19th-century village rather than building a thin Halloween event beside it. Families can explore the historic homes, farms, trades, gallery and nature trails while the museum adds its gentler spooky-season material. The same day also includes a traditional kiln firing. For a bigger family story, the museum's separately registered overnight runs from 5 p.m. Saturday to 10 a.m. Sunday with historical games, a Jack-o'-lantern nightlight craft, a slightly spooky village tour, campfire treats, breakfast and a morning foliage hike.

DriveAbout 1 hr 25–35 min
Day eventSaturday 10–4
OvernightSat. 5 p.m.–Sun. 10 a.m.

Why it earns the drive: Even if one special activity misses, there is still a full living-history museum to explore. That makes it more resilient than a small Halloween event, especially for mixed-age families.

Book/check first: October tickets can sell quickly, so check the official spooky-season page before leaving. The overnight requires advance registration; do not assume walk-up space. If you go for the daytime program, use our family museum-day system and stop while the kids still want one more building.

3
Best foliage spectacle · Canandaigua

Bristol Mountain Fall Festival and Sky Ride

The chairlift is the reason to make the drive: it turns fall color into an experience rather than a windshield view. The festival adds live music, food and artisan vendors, summit walks, apple-and-cider samples and optional activities for children, including a kids adventure park for ages 4–7 plus rock-wall and bungee-trampoline options for ages 4 and up. Saturday's current sunny 72°F forecast is nearly ideal.

DriveAbout 1 hr 35–50 min
HoursSat. & Sun. 11–5
Sky ride ticket$20 ages 2+

The honest fit: Choose this when everyone is comfortable on an open chairlift and the foliage view is the main event. General admission without the lift is $12 for ages 12 and up, but at that point LaFayette or Grisamore is the better value.

Book/check first: Reserve Fall Sky Ride admission through the official festival page; Bristol explicitly recommends advance purchase. Fall Sky Ride admission includes the festival, is free under age 2 and is weather-dependent. Confirm lift status the morning of the trip and bring a secure layer for the cooler ride down.

4
Most unusual Sunday · Utica

Adirondack Railroad Fall Gateway

This is the no-hike foliage option: board at Utica's Union Station and spend about five hours riding vintage coaches through the Adirondacks toward Thendara and back. Sunday's published schedule departs Utica at 11:30 a.m., reaches Thendara about 1:30 p.m., turns at 2:15 and returns around 4:30. Passengers do not get off at Thendara, so treat it as a long scenic train ride—not an Old Forge visit.

Drive to stationAbout 55–65 minutes
Train timeAbout 5 hours Sunday
Coach fare$55 adult; $40 ages 2–12

Why it belongs here: The journey itself is the attraction, and the current Utica forecast is comfortable with clouds and sun. It is a strong fit for train-loving children, grandparents or anyone who wants fall color without stairs and steep trail miles.

Book/check first: Check October 11 inventory and class availability on the official Fall Gateway page before building the day around it. Diamond class is already marked sold out on the current page. Confirm accessibility needs by phone; wheelchair seating has specific size limits and onboard restrooms are not wheelchair accessible. Use our Adirondacks family guide for preparation, not for adding stops to this no-layover excursion.

5
Best free-entry farm day · Locke

Grisamore Farms Fall Festival

Grisamore delivers the classic farm-day pieces without an admission or parking charge: pick-your-own apples and pumpkins, a sunflower maze, wagon rides, farm animals, pumpkin and face painting, U-cut flowers, vendors, food and live music from 11 to 2 both days. It is easy to scale from a two-hour outing to most of an afternoon.

DriveAbout 45–60 minutes
HoursSat. 8–5; Sun. 10–5
Admission/parkingFree

Best use: Pick this over LaFayette if the family wants farm space, fruit and a slower pace instead of a packed midway and hundreds of craft booths. Apples, pumpkins, flowers, food and some activities still cost extra, so “free admission” should not become “no budget.”

Check first: Review current picking conditions on the official Fall Festival page. Bring your own wagon if you have one; the farm says its supply is limited. Decide before arrival whether the family is buying produce, doing paid crafts or simply using the free maze and wagon ride.

6
Best pure outdoor day · Watkins Glen

Watkins Glen Gorge Before Seasonal Closing

This is not a festival, and that is the point. The Gorge Trail's 19 waterfalls, stone tunnels and stairways are especially good in fall, and New York State Parks currently lists an October 25 seasonal closing—earlier if repeated overnight temperatures in the 30s force it. The weekend shuttle is still scheduled during the fall operating period, giving families the option to walk one direction rather than immediately reverse the stairs.

DriveAbout 1 hr 30–45 min
Park hoursDawn to dusk
Current fee$10 vehicle; shuttle $6 each way

Best version: Go Saturday morning. Watkins Glen is currently forecast mostly sunny near 71°F Saturday, while Sunday has afternoon-rain risk. Wear shoes with wet-stone grip and expect many stairs; pets are not allowed on the Gorge Trail.

Check first: Confirm same-day trail and shuttle operation on the official park page before the 90-minute drive. Decide whether to pay for the shuttle before starting. Turn around early if the stairs stop being fun—the best family hike ends with reserve energy, not a forced march.

My honest ranking for your family

  1. LaFayette Saturday near opening — the strongest mix of local convenience, kid anchors and adult interest.
  2. Genesee Country Village Saturday — the richest full-day outing, especially if the overnight still has space.
  3. Bristol Mountain Saturday — the memorable foliage play when everyone wants the chairlift.
  4. Adirondack Railroad Sunday — unusually good for train kids and low-walking multigenerational groups.
  5. Grisamore Sunday — the lower-cost, lower-pressure farm alternative.
  6. Watkins Glen Saturday morning — the best active day, but only for a family ready for wet stone and stairs.

If it were my weekend, I would go to LaFayette shortly after 9 a.m. Saturday, then keep Sunday deliberately simple at Grisamore. If the kids unanimously want one big story instead, book Bristol's sky ride for Saturday or the Adirondack train for Sunday now—those are the two plans most vulnerable to capacity.
